<Aside>
	The mechs are the one thing every person at the crossing has a different
	answer about. This is the first quest that treats that as a subject rather
	than as scenery.
</Aside>

## Quest Overview

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Giver" icon="person">
		Tam on the bank. Turned in to the mage.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Reward" icon="star">
		80 experience, and two points of standing with Tam, who was right to be
		listened to.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## Steps to Completion

<Steps>
1. **Ask Tam about the walking machines**
   His guess sets `tam_guessed_the_mechs`.
2. **Put it to the mage**
   Whose answer sets `mage_weighed_the_guess`.
</Steps>
