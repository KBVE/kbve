<Aside>
	The first thing anybody in Friendslop asks you for, and the cheapest lesson in
	the game: the people here want something, and they will tell you what if you
	stand still long enough to be told.
</Aside>

## Quest Overview

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Giver" icon="person">
		Marlow, the bridge keeper, at the bank end of the span.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Reward" icon="star">
		40 experience, and two points of standing with Marlow — enough that he
		greets you as a regular afterwards.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## Steps to Completion

<Steps>
1. **Talk to Marlow**
   He is beside the crossing, facing the deck.
2. **Ask about the toll, and pay it**
   Paying sets `marlow_toll_paid`, which is also what opens his question about
   the mechs that come down from the ridge.
</Steps>
