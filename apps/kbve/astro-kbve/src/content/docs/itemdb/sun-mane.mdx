## Overview

<ItemDBPanel data={frontmatter} />

Used on a [Shibe](/docs/npcdb/shibe/) from the pet hub to evolve it into
[Solmane](/docs/npcdb/solmane/), a fire form. The item is consumed only if the
evolution actually happens.

<Aside type="caution">
	Evolution is permanent and a Shibe can only do it once. This item chooses the form.
</Aside>
