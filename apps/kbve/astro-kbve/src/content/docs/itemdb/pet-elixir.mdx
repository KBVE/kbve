## Overview

Restores one pet's HP and move PP to full. Pets carry their battle damage and spent PP
between duels, so an elixir (or a visit to a healer) is how a worn-down roster gets back
into fighting shape.

<Aside type="note">
	The elixir is spent from the pet hub, not the hotbar — it targets a roster slot
	rather than the player.
</Aside>
