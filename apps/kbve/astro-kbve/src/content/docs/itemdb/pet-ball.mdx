## Overview

<ItemDBPanel data={frontmatter} />

Thrown at a wild pet during a duel to try to catch it. The odds scale with how weakened the
target is: a pet at full health is roughly three times harder to catch than one on its last
legs, and a status effect roughly doubles the chance again.

<Aside type="caution">
	The ball is spent on the attempt, not on the result — a throw that breaks out is gone.
	Weaken the target first.
</Aside>
