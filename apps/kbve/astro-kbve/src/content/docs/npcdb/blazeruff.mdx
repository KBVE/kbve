One of the eighteen forms a [Shibe](/docs/npcdb/shibe/) can take. Evolution is a
one-way, one-time change, triggered by using a **Ember Shard**
on the pet — a Shibe that becomes this is never anything else.

<Aside type="note" title="Terminal form">
	This species does not evolve further. Its stat line, movepool and element are what the
	Shibe permanently committed to.
</Aside>
