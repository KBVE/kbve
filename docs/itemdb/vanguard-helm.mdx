## Overview

The Vanguard Helm is the anchor piece of the Vanguard set; three points of armor for four points of weight, which is a trade most people make once and then stop thinking about. 
It occupies a 2×2 block in the pack, so it travels worn far more often than it travels carried.

The visor is the whole design argument. Closed, it costs peripheral vision and rewards you for looking directly at the thing trying to kill you. Open, it is a steel hat.

## In the field

- Pairs with the [Vanguard Breastplate](/itemdb/vanguard-breastplate/) and [Pauldrons](/itemdb/vanguard-pauldrons/) before the lighter pieces; head and chest cover the hits you do not see coming
- The liner absorbs water and stays heavy for a day — dry it upright, not on its crown
- Covers hair and most headgear-slot cosmetics while worn

## Acquisition

Garrison quartermasters stock it at standing rate, no rank required. It also turns up in patrol wreckage often enough that most squads own one nobody paid for.
