## Overview

One point of armor for one point of weight, in a 2×1 footprint that fits the awkward gap most packs have left over. The Impact set is the cheap floor of protection: nothing here saves you from a real hit, but the whole kit costs less than one Vanguard piece and never slows you down.

Shoulder pads sit in the chest slot, which surprises people. They stack with nothing else on the torso.

## In the field

- Best on runners and gatherers — the profile stays low and the weight stays honest
- Common starter pairing: [Impact Elbow Pads](/itemdb/impact-elbow-pads/) and [Impact Knee Pads](/itemdb/impact-knee-pads/), upgrading the chest first
- Polymer cracks in the cold instead of bending; check the seams after a winter run

## Acquisition

Every surplus stall in the district has a crate of them. Craftable from salvaged polymer for anyone unwilling to pay forty.
